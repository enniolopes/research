# Phase 8 — Publication

Sources located 2026-09-10, not yet read at source. Policies change: re-check the
venue's and funder's current text before each submission.

**When this applies.** Anything leaves the repository for a venue, a funder, a repository
of record or the public.

**What it requires.**

- **Preprint with versioning**; each version's DOI recorded in the protocol.
- **Code and aggregates deposited with a DOI** (Zenodo or equivalent), FAIR: findable
  identifier, access conditions stated, open formats, licence and provenance included.
- **Data management plan** in the funder's shape — check the current text; as an example,
  FAPESP requires ≤2 pages: data and metadata
  produced, legal/ethical restrictions, privacy and confidentiality policy, preservation
  and sharing policy, embargo).
- **AI-use declaration** in the venue's shape — check the current text; two examples.
  Elsevier: a fixed section before the
  references stating tool and purpose, with the authors' responsibility for the content.
  SciELO: tool and version, purpose, limits of the contribution, impact on writing and
  analysis, ethical compliance. AI is never an author.
- **PII exposure resolved**: nothing deposited identifies a household or a person; the
  disclosure floor from phase 4 is re-checked on the deposited aggregates.
- **Decisions logged**: venue chosen and why, licence, embargo — each with a revision
  condition.

**The error it prevents.** A deposit that cannot be cited; a data plan written after the
data; an undeclared AI contribution; an identifiable row in a public table.

**Exit gate.** DOIs recorded in the protocol for preprint, code and aggregates; DMP and
AI declaration exist in the venue's shape; PII check on the deposit passed.

**Sources.** FAPESP, *Gestão de dados* (mandatory PGD since 2020-09-01). Elsevier,
generative-AI policy for authors. SciELO 2023, guide for the use of AI in research
communication. Wilkinson et al. 2016 (FAIR).
