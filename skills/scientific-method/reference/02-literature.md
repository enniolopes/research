# Phase 2 — Literature

Use before protocol freeze and whenever a material claim about prior knowledge enters the work.

- Search at the level needed for the claim; record database/source, query and date when claiming a gap or coverage.
- Retrieve and read the content that supports each proposition. DOI/landing-page resolution is identity, not support.
- Mark abstract/excerpt-only evidence as such.
- Bound negative claims: “not found in this search” is not “does not exist”.
- Before calling studies contradictory or independent, compare constructs, measures, populations, conditions and shared data/source lineage.

Exit when sources actually used are inspectable at the level claimed and the knowledge gap is stated with its search limits.
