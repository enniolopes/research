# Problem statement

Gate 1A keeps the question compact and falsifiable. The protocol section pointed to by `RESEARCH.map` contains these validator-checked fields:

```markdown
## Question
<one sentence>

Claim: descriptive | associational | causal
Unit of analysis: <unit>
Estimand: <target population, contrast/exposure if applicable, outcome, time, summary>
Refutation: <observation that would make the proposed question/premise wrong or materially narrower>
Objection: <strongest obvious design/measurement/selection objection and where it will be addressed>
Who cares: <decision or knowledge consequence and its owner>
Non-goals: <what this study will not answer>
```

For causal claims, add the intervention/contrast and identification assumptions needed to interpret the estimand causally; a target-trial description is useful when it clarifies those assumptions, not a mandatory field for descriptive/associational work.

The statement is a working scientific commitment, not a grant proposal. Feasibility, novelty and alternative lineages may be recorded when material, but are not required merely to fill a template.
