# Phase 6 — Writing

Write from committed evidence, not memory.

- Every material computed number comes from a named aggregate/result; include uncertainty when the quantity/design requires it, not mechanically for exact counts or identifiers.
- Define variables as their source defines them and surface material ambiguity.
- Figures identify their producing aggregate/code.
- Claim wording stays within `May claim / May not claim` and receives direct claim→result lineage when material.
- Limitations come from known assumptions, failed checks and `NOT_VERIFIED` evidence rather than being invented at the end.
- Abstracts/slides/summaries are projections of the same current claims, not independent editions.

Exit when material numbers and claims trace to evidence, figures are reproducible from named artifacts, and interpretation does not exceed the design.
