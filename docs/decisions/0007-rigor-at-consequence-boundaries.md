# ADR 0007 — Put rigor at consequence boundaries

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Preserve broad creative freedom during exploration and impose mandatory controls at high-consequence boundaries: scientific commitment, confirmatory result exposure, post-freeze plan changes, material claims and release/review.

## Rationale

Continuous schema/checklist enforcement can reduce useful exploration and consume model attention. Uncontrolled transitions, however, create p-hacking, hindsight and unsupported-claim failures.

## Consequences

- exploration may generate speculative branches without canonicalizing all of them;
- preflights/gates are explicit and mechanically enforced where possible;
- a new control must name the failure mechanism it prevents.
